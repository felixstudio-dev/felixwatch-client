# Support and Lifecycle Policy

No public Felix Watch Client versions or support/EOL deadlines are established.
Future published releases must explicitly identify CURRENT, SUPPORTED,
SECURITY_ONLY, DEPRECATED or EOL status; approved platform coverage; security
fix scope; migration path; and a support end date or **TBD — not yet approved**.

No automatic indefinite-support promise is made. Installation and incident
recovery remain the operator's controlled responsibilities until documented
and approved per component. A Client package never authorizes automatic
remote remediation, SSH or deploy.
